Our Story

Cloth, fit and family.

A shop that began in 1980 and still treats every bride like a guest at home.

Started with a good eye for cloth

Nasim Aapa opened her doors in 1980 with one counter. Word travelled the way it does in the old city, from one family to the next.

Today three branches carry bridal lehengas, dress materials and readymades, and our tailors still cut every custom order by hand.
